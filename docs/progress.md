- B2/C7 debugging uncovered a consistent late-March mismatch pattern on exact workbook branches: `XLF 3d LT15`, `EDIV 6d LT33`, `FEZ 21d LT45`, `GSY 3d LT70`, and `PXH 11d LT36` all appear earlier in B2 with matching trade cycles, but our replay produces one additional late cycle (`3/10→3/23`, `3/18→3/23`, or `3/20→3/23`) that B2 does not log. - Auditing a 15-branch famous-ticker shortlist against C7 showed that branch-level 2025 IS metrics are very close to workbook values when the exact branch exists in C7, so metric math is not the main problem there. The interesting exclusions were upstream/selector-driven instead: `ARKK 15d LT33` fails our liquidity filter, `GLD 7d LT37` and `FXI 15d LT42` lose to lower-DD alternatives under our selector, `EEM 3d LT10` loses to a same-DD higher-profit branch, and `XLF 3d LT15` loses to `XLF 3d LT14` because of a microscopic floating-point DD edge.
- Identified a concrete bug in our liquidity implementation: `passes_liquidity_filter(...)` currently requires the 90-day average dollar volume (`Close * Volume`) to stay above $100k for every valid 90-day window across the ticker's full history. That incorrectly excludes names like ARKK because of early-history illiquidity even though their 2025 IS-period liquidity is far above the threshold. The intended fix is to evaluate liquidity per rolling window using the IS year / IS end date rather than the full history.
- Implemented the liquidity fix in `system30/`: universe loading now only gates on inception, a new `passes_liquidity_filter_asof(...)` checks trailing 90D dollar volume as of each IS year-end, and the offline selector applies liquidity per rolling window instead of globally across full ticker history. Sanity checks confirmed ARKK now passes liquidity at 2025-12-31 (~$742.5M ADV90) and remains in the candidate universe.
- A 2026-only post-fix offline rerun under `system30_output/c7_liquidity_fix/` selected 1,359 branches (up from the buggy 718) and now includes ARKK, though our selected ARKK branch is `11d RSI ARKK LT28 - L ARKK` rather than workbook C7's `15d RSI ARKK LT33`, so fixing liquidity removes one exclusion bug but does not by itself explain all remaining C7 differences.
